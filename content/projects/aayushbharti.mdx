## Why I Built This

I wanted my portfolio to double as a product case-study platform with reusable systems and scalable content.

## Architecture Overview

The platform combines section-based pages, dynamic route content, and consistent visual language across pages.

![Platform screenshot](/projects-images/portfolio/screen1.webp)

## Key Decisions

- Design data-driven pages first, then iterate visual layers.
- Keep content and presentation loosely coupled.
- Optimize loading and typography for long-form reading.

## What I Learned

Product-level thinking improves personal sites dramatically when consistency and iteration are prioritized.
