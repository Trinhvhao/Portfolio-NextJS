## Why I Built This

I wanted to build a practical utility where performance, processing reliability, and clean UX matter equally.

## How It Works

Users upload media, choose conversion settings, and download transformed output through a streamlined interface.

![Flux Lura interface](/projects-images/flux-lura/screen1.jpg)

## Key Decisions

- Keep transformation flows understandable for non-technical users.
- Separate UI orchestration from media processing logic.
- Optimize for fast feedback during conversion steps.

## What I Learned

Utility products succeed when friction is removed from every step.
