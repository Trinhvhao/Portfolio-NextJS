## Why I Built This

I wanted to explore how course discovery and learning flows can be simplified for both students and instructors.

## How It Works

Users can explore learning paths, navigate course content, and interact with instructor-centered workflows.

![Zenith Minds preview](/projects-images/zenith-minds/screen1.jpg)

## Key Decisions

- Use modular content blocks for scalable course pages.
- Keep navigation shallow to reduce user confusion.
- Prioritize readability and onboarding clarity.

## What I Learned

Education products need both technical quality and pedagogical clarity to feel effective.
