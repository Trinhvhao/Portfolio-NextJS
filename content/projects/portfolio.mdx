## Why I Built This

I wanted a portfolio that works as a real product platform, not just a static profile page.

## Architecture Overview

The app combines content publishing, dynamic routing, and reusable UI systems under a consistent design language.

![Portfolio screen](/projects-images/portfolio/screen1.webp)

## Key Decisions

- Build reusable sections and data contracts first.
- Keep performance and readability as first-class priorities.
- Treat content updates like product updates.

## What I Learned

Long-term maintainability comes from information architecture as much as visual polish.
