## Why I Built This

I wanted to design an EdTech product that supports both learners and creators with clear role-based workflows.

## How It Works

Students discover and consume courses while instructors manage content, enrollments, and pricing.

## Key Decisions

- Keep dashboard interactions role-specific.
- Separate course delivery from commerce flows.
- Ensure API contracts are stable for admin and learner surfaces.

## What I Learned

In education products, trust comes from smooth learning flow and predictable content access.
